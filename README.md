# 🏛️ IN-TUT: Comprehensive Institute Management System

**IN-TUT** is a robust, full-stack management platform designed to streamline academic operations. Built with a focus on modular architecture and real-time data tracking, it serves as a central hub for Students, Staff, and Head of Departments (HOD).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🔐 Multi-User Authentication
* **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Distinct dashboards for HOD, Staff, and Students.
* **Custom User Model:** Extends Django's `AbstractUser` to support profile-specific metadata and pictures.

### 📅 Academic & Course Management
* **Dynamic Academic Years:** Manage start and end dates with real-time tracking of creation and updates.
* **Course & Subject Mapping:** Advanced relational mapping between Branches, Subjects, and specialized Staff members.

### 📝 Attendance & Notifications
* **Digital Attendance:** Automated attendance reporting for subjects and individual student tracking.
* **Smart Notifications:** Real-time messaging system for Students and Staff to stay updated on institute activities.

### 🏥 Leave & Feedback Management
* **Automated Workflow:** Request and approve leaves through a dedicated status-tracking system.
* **Feedback Loops:** Direct communication channels for students and staff to provide structured feedback to the administration.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Stack

* **Backend Framework:** Django (Python 3.13+)
* **Database:** SQLite (Relational structure with 10+ interconnected models)
* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap
* **Logic:** Custom Python backend for role-based routing and automated timestamping.
